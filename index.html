Essay practice.

Start with what you know and finish with a full essay in your own words. You revise the content, plan the essay, write it one paragraph at a time, and get feedback on each part before you move on.

The question you will answer

Causes of the First World War. Aim to write the full essay in about 45 minutes.

Written for the IB Diploma History course. Not produced or endorsed by the International Baccalaureate Organization.

How it works
1

Understand and revise

Work out what the question is asking. Then revise the five causes of the First World War, test yourself on each one from memory, and meet what historians argue.

2

Match and decide

Match each cause to what it did. Weigh the causes, decide your thesis and recall what you missed.

3

Plan, write and check

Write your introduction. Then plan and write one paragraph at a time, and finish with your conclusion. Check each part and fix it.

4

Keep your essay

Copy your finished essay or download it as a file, ready to hand in.
